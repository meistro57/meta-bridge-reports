## The Core Claim  
Fundamental reality is a singular, non-dual field prior to subject-object distinctions; apparent separation is an emergent illusion or contextual overlay. This indivisible ground manifests as both consciousness and form without inherent division.  

## The Receipts  

**Tibetan Book of the Dead (8th-14th c., Vajrayana Buddhism)**  
Frames non-duality through the dissolution of egoic perception at death. The "clear light mind" (Dharmakaya) is presented as the primordial state where observer-observed distinctions collapse. This maps to the core claim by treating duality as a transient cognitive artifact, not ontological truth.  

**The Ra Contact (1981, Channeled Cosmology)**  
Describes non-duality as an infinite unity ("Intelligent Infinity") expressing through fractalized consciousness. Separation arises from free will exploring limitation, but remains illusory against the absolute. This mirrors Advaita's *maya* by framing individuation as self-imposed constraint within unity.  

**Doctrine of Maya (Advaita Vedanta, 8th c.)**  
Posits Brahman as sole reality, with individual selves (Atman) identical to it. Duality emerges from ignorance (*avidya*) superimposing distinctions onto the non-dual substrate. Its mechanism—"superimposition and negation"—directly equates apparent multiplicity with misperception.  

**Bashar Transcripts (1984-present, Channeled Philosophy)**  
States reality is fundamentally unified consciousness experiencing itself through perspective. Separation is defined as a belief structure generating holographic fragmentation. This operationalizes non-duality by treating duality as a functional tool for experience, not an inherent condition.  

**Bohm's *Wholeness and the Implicate Order* (1980, Quantum Physics)**  
Rejects Cartesian splits via an "implicate order" where consciousness and matter unfold from an undivided whole. Non-locality and quantum entanglement demonstrate his claim that apparent separateness is a derivative explicate pattern. This grounds non-duality in physical law.  

**Gospel of Thomas (1st c., Gnosticism)**  
Logion 22 resolves dualities ("male-female, eye-hand") into a primordial "single one." Enlightenment requires transcending perceptual opposites to access undivided being. Its dialectical method converges with Advaita's *neti neti* by treating duality as a perceptual hurdle to overcome.
