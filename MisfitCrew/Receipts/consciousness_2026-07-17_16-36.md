## Why The Convergence Matters  
Independent agreement across neuroscience, esotericism, mysticism, and systems theory suggests consciousness operates as a cosmic primitive—not emergent from matter, but antecedent to it. For this pattern to hold, consciousness must be: (1) non-local, avoiding cultural or disciplinary boundaries; (2) subject to invariant laws permitting cross-traditional verification; and (3) sufficiently plastic to manifest as emotion, sacredness, or quantum potential without contradiction. This forces a reevaluation of materialism: if consciousness is foundational, reality itself becomes epistemic.

## The Hardware Glitch  
The claim self-destructs under three tensions:  
1. **Infinite regress**: If consciousness is fundamental (Bashar, Encyclopedia), what instantiates it? Appeals to "self-existence" merely restate the problem.  
2. **Metric collapse**: Evolution toward "higher" consciousness (Gurdjieff, Bohm) lacks an objective yardstick. Who observes the increase, and from what vantage?  
3. **Tautological agency**: Gazzaniga and James expose a catch-22—if consciousness "selects" content but has no uncaused volition, selection reduces to determinism, voiding the claim’s core premise of participatory evolution.

## The Operative Question
