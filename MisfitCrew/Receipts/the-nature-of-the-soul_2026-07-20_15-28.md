Its insistence on the soul’s inviolability structurally mirrors Platonic forms (the soul participates in eternal ideals) while resolving action/non-action paradoxes: the soul acts without being acted upon, anchoring identity in being rather than doing.

This introduces a structural tension: renewal implies change, yet presupposes an enduring core. It converges with palingenesis (spiritual rebirth) and karmic cleansing models, framing transformation not as alteration of essence but as removal of obscurations.
