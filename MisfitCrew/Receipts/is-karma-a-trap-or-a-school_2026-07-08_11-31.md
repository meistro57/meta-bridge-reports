## The Core Claim  
Karma operates as a recursive learning mechanism for consciousness evolution, not a punitive moral ledger. Its cycles persist until core lessons—psychological integration, behavioral pattern interruption, or trauma resolution—are consciously mastered. Transcendence occurs through recognizing its voluntary nature.  

## The Receipts  
**Dolores Cannon (1993-2010s):** Consistently reframed karma as soul-level trauma healing across 8+ texts. In *Convoluted Universe 5*, declared it an "optional habit cycle" transcendable via awareness. Maps to Jungian shadow work by treating recurrence as unresolved psychic material demanding integration.  
**Bashar/Darryl Anka (1984-present):** Defined karma as self-generated "momentum," denying external enforcement. Aligns with non-dual axioms: consequences are projections of inner state, not cosmic justice. Makes karma synonymous with unconscious creation.  
**Ra Contact (1981):** Framed karma as "energetic inertia" resolved via forgiveness. Positions pain as catalytic but non-guaranteed growth fuel. Mirrors Stoic *amor fati* while adding cessation mechanics—directly answering Cannon's "optional" claim with actionable release protocols.  
**Seth/Jane Roberts (1974):** Exposed punitive karma as a tool for social oppression. Highlights how "cosmic justice" narratives justify hierarchy. Converges with Cannon's therapeutic model by divorcing karma from morality, anchoring it in self-created limitation patterns instead.  
**Secret Doctrine (1888):** Called karma the universe's "fundamental law" while paradoxically describing "cumulative burdens." Reveals the core tension: Is it immutable physics or educative software? Cannon and Bashar resolve this by making participation elective post-awakening.  
**Plato (4th c. BCE):** Positioned suffering as soul pedagogy for wisdom acquisition. Established the "earth as school" archetype later expanded in Nag Hammadi and Urantia. His *anamnesis* (recollection) is Cannon's "lesson repetition" with Hellenic rigor.  

## Why The Convergence Matters  
These sources—spanning 2,400 years, from Athenian philosophy to modern channeling—independently reduce karma to *experiential recursion until mastery*. The pattern suggests consciousness evolution inherently requires feedback loops. Retributive interpretations emerge not from the mechanism itself, but from cultural superimposition of justice frameworks onto a neutral learning algorithm. Convergence implies karma's structure is a root feature of embodied existence, while its moral packaging is optional.  

## The Hardware Glitch  
**Volitional paradox:** If karma is optional (Cannon, Bashar), why do ascended beings still engage it? If fundamental (Secret Doctrine), how is transcendence possible? This either implies 1) a developmental threshold where the law becomes elective, or 2) a contradiction in the model.  
**Enforcement dilemma:** Self-created karma (Bashar) requires no external enforcer, yet karmic consequences *feel* exogenous. Subjective experience clashes with non-dual claims of total self-authority.  
**Justice hijacking:** Seth's critique manifests when therapeutic karma is weaponized into social control ("your poverty is karmic debt"). The educative mechanism becomes oppression infrastructure when detached from sovereignty.  

## The Operative Question  
What behavioral or perceptual shift signals the transition from karma-as-required-curriculum to karma-as-elective-simulation—and what operational mode supersedes it?
